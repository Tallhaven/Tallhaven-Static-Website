OfferCheck Cebu Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Tallhaven provides OfferCheck Cebu, an offline tool for comparing job offers and planning work-related costs. For support or privacy questions, contact seththedeveloper@gmail.com.

Information processed on your device

You may enter salary figures, offer aliases, employment phases, deductions, benefits, work costs, notes, recruiter questions, comparison snapshots and first-payday planning details. OfferCheck Cebu processes and stores these entries on your device. We do not receive them through the app and do not operate a server that collects them.

The Android app stores saved entries in an encrypted local database. Its encryption key is kept using the operating system's secure storage. Android backup is disabled for the app. These protections reduce exposure but do not guarantee security against every device or operating-system threat.

If you enable the optional app lock, your operating system handles device authentication, including supported biometrics or passcode fallback. OfferCheck Cebu does not receive or store your fingerprints, face templates or device passcode.

Accounts, tracking and learning references

OfferCheck Cebu has no account registration, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, subscription, payment processing or AI service. Core calculations and included learning references work offline. The app does not fetch reference updates in the background; updated references arrive through app releases.

Sharing and external services

Sharing is optional. The app shows the text for you to review before opening your device's share sheet. Only the text you choose to share is passed to the destination app or recipient you select. Shared text can contain personal figures, aliases and details you entered. Copies held by recipients or other apps are outside OfferCheck Cebu's control.

When you choose an external source link or support email, your browser, email app or destination service handles the interaction under its own policies. If you email us, we receive the message and information you choose to include. We use it to respond and address the request, retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations, and do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Google Play and your device operating system may process download, purchase-free installation, diagnostics or other platform information under their own privacy policies. This is separate from OfferCheck Cebu's local processing.

Retention and deletion

Saved app entries remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Use Settings → Delete All to remove the app's local database, encryption key, lock preference and app-owned temporary exports. Deleting a single offer removes that offer and its revisions; saved comparison snapshots remain until Delete All. Copies you shared or copied elsewhere must be managed separately.

For information you sent to us by email, you may request access or deletion at seththedeveloper@gmail.com. We may retain information where required by law or necessary to address an unresolved request.

Children

OfferCheck Cebu is intended for adult job applicants and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's practices change. The effective date above will identify the current version. Contact seththedeveloper@gmail.com with privacy questions.
