Privacy Policy

Geometry Smash: Shape Battles
Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Tallhaven LLC handles information when you use Geometry Smash on Android or iOS. It covers solo play, direct Wi-Fi play, Bluetooth play, and Internet multiplayer.

Solo play and information on your device

You can play solo without creating an account. The current app does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, or an analytics SDK. Gameplay and control settings are handled on your device. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or payment information to play.

Nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth multiplayer

When you choose nearby multiplayer, the app discovers other players and exchanges room information, fighter choices, match settings, gameplay inputs, and connection-status information with the other device. Nearby discovery may expose a room name or device name and uses network addresses or Bluetooth identifiers to connect. These exchanges support the match; the nearby mode does not send gameplay through our Internet relay.

Bluetooth and local-network permissions are used to discover and connect to nearby players. On older Android versions, the operating system requires location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app uses that permission for nearby-device discovery and does not read GPS coordinates. You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings; the affected multiplayer mode may then be unavailable.

Internet multiplayer

When you choose an Internet room, connection requests and gameplay messages pass through a Cloudflare-hosted relay. They include a room code, host or guest role, fighter and match choices, gameplay inputs, and connection information. Cloudflare receives network information such as your IP address to provide the service. The app uses secure WebSocket connections to the relay.

The relay code forwards match messages and keeps active room connections in memory. It does not write match histories to a persistent database. Infrastructure providers may process operational or security logs under their own service settings and retention practices. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Sharing and purposes

Multiplayer information is exchanged with the other player to run your match and, for Internet play, processed by the relay provider to deliver messages. We do not sell personal information or use gameplay data for targeted advertising. Apple and Google separately process store downloads, purchases if applicable, and platform diagnostics under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

The app has no player-account database to delete. Active match connections end when you leave or disconnect; the relay has no persistent match-history storage. You can stop nearby discovery by leaving multiplayer or revoking permissions. Remove the app to remove its local app data. If you email support, your message and email address are retained in the support mailbox as needed to respond and resolve the request. You may ask us to delete support correspondence; some records may need to be retained for legal obligations. Service-provider logs follow the provider's retention practices.

Security

We limit information handling to the features described here. Internet relay traffic uses transport encryption. Nearby traffic is exchanged directly using the device's networking and Bluetooth facilities. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

The app does not require personal profiles or knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support mailbox, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes and will update the date above. For questions, privacy requests, or deletion of support messages, contact Tallhaven LLC at seththedeveloper@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents.
